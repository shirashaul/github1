## 1. What Zuckerberg and Meta executives have said (2025–2026) about AI in WhatsApp: personal superintelligence, assistant, agents, memory, proactive AI

### Takeaway
Meta's stated direction moved through three steps. July 2025 brought "personal superintelligence for everyone", with glasses as the primary device. Q4 2025 and Q1 2026 said "agents really work" and new models were coming. Q2 2026 (around July 29, 2026) went further: WhatsApp is the main place people use Meta AI, and messaging becomes *more* important as people use several agents. Meta then shipped **Muse**, a personal agent that takes actions and is reachable inside WhatsApp, on Sept 8, 2026 (US only).

## 2. Meta's models in 2026 (Muse Spark, Muse Wonder, Llama status) and how they power WhatsApp

### Takeaway
Meta Superintelligence Labs (MSL, led by Alexandr Wang) replaced Llama as Meta's frontier line with the closed **Muse** family. **Muse Spark** (April 8, 2026) now powers Meta AI in WhatsApp, including group chats, from about May 12, 2026. **Muse Wonder / Wonder Full** (October 12, 2026) is an agent-focused frontier model. It goes first to paying Muse subscribers, and no date has been given for free Meta AI users in WhatsApp.

### Inferences
- **Tiering pattern:** free Meta AI in WhatsApp gets the "Spark"-class model, and paying users get the "Wonder"-class model with agent abilities. For a WhatsApp user, the strongest agentic capabilities are likely to arrive behind Muse subscriptions first and reach free Meta AI later, if at all. Meta has given no date.
- Moving to closed models gives Meta tighter control over what runs in WhatsApp. That fits the Private Processing and agent-safety story, and it also suits the paid-tier strategy.

## 3. Business AI on WhatsApp and monetization (Business Agent, click-to-WhatsApp, ads in Meta AI, subscriptions, Updates-tab ads)

### Takeaway
Business AI is the most mature and most monetized part of WhatsApp AI. **Meta Business Agent** went global in June 2026, has been billed per token since August 1, 2026, and is used by more than 1M businesses weekly. Consumer monetization now has three parts: Meta AI chats feed ad targeting on FB/IG (Dec 2025), Status/Updates-tab ads (global, with the EU in 2026), and paid tiers. Paid tiers include **Meta One** (Sept 15, 2026: $2.99 WhatsApp Plus up to $19.99 Premium AI) and **Muse** ($20 or $100 per month). Basic Meta AI stays free.

### Inferences
- The business model is visibly diversifying away from "ads only". There are four revenue lines: (1) businesses pay per AI token or conversation on WhatsApp, (2) ads in the Updates tab plus AI-informed targeting elsewhere, (3) consumer subscriptions (Meta One, Muse), and (4) enterprise sales. WhatsApp sits at the center of (1) and is a surface for (3).

### Inferences
- Meta's actual agent architecture keeps the agent in its own sandbox (VM plus connectors), with WhatsApp as the conversation channel. It is not an agent embedded in your E2EE chats. Agent actions on your WhatsApp chats themselves (auto-replying, managing groups) would collide with E2EE and Private Processing limits. A true "AI group admin" or "reply on my behalf" would be a further step, and no source shows it on the roadmap.
