# Meta's policy direction on third-party AI in WhatsApp, and how regulation is changing it (as of 2026-10-14)

Research date: 2026-10-14. This builds on two earlier notes: `research_notes/מזכירה אישית בוואטסאפ לאייפון/cloud_api_coexistence.md` (section 8) and `unofficial_readers_risks.md` (sections 2 and 5). Facts already established there are referenced briefly, not repeated.

**Method caveat:** the research environment's egress proxy blocked direct page fetches for nearly every relevant domain (ec.europa.eu, whatsapp.com, techcrunch.com, tlt.com, euperspectives.eu, techtimes.com, webonwhatsapp.com, about.fb.com). Facts below therefore come from search-engine extracts and summaries of the cited pages, not full-page reads. Where only one secondary or vendor source supports a claim, it is labelled. Labels used:
- **[confirmed]**: multiple independent outlets, or an official body or the party itself.
- **[reported]**: a single credible outlet or law firm.
- **[vendor/blog]**: a single vendor or SEO blog.
- **[speculative]**: forward-looking.

**Bottom line for the report writer:** in 2026 WhatsApp is **closed by default and open only where a regulator forces it**. In January 2026 Meta shut general-purpose AI assistants (ChatGPT, Copilot, Perplexity, Luzia, Poke) out of the Business API worldwide. It reopened, under compulsion, in Italy (Dec 2025), Brazil (Jan 2026; fee-based since March), the EEA (fee-based from March 2026, free from the 9 June 2026 interim order; ChatGPT back on 13 July 2026) and probably Turkey (interim order 5 June 2026). Meta is appealing the EU order (Aug 2026). In Sept 2026 it reportedly rewrote the carve-out so that it applies only in countries "Meta lists in its developer documentation", meaning only where it is legally required. No Israeli regulatory action was found. For a user in Israel (+972 number), the global ban applies in full. Meta is meanwhile building its own assistant (Meta AI in Hebrew since June 2026, with Gmail/Calendar connectors since July 2026), pricing its own Business Agent separately from third-party AI, and tightening device linking (passkey gate, ~June 2026). Every vector points toward a more closed platform outside regulated markets.

## 1. The WhatsApp Business Solution Terms "AI Providers" rule: scope, who left, rationale, 2026 changes, personal bots

### Takeaway
The clause bans AI providers from the Business Platform when a general-purpose AI assistant is the *primary* product, "as determined by Meta in its sole discretion". It hit all existing users on 15 Jan 2026, and ChatGPT, Copilot, Perplexity, Luzia and Poke left. In 2026 Meta did not soften the rule. It added carve-outs only where regulators forced them: first named as the EEA and Brazil, then reportedly (23 Sep 2026) as "certain countries listed in developer docs". It also layered on pricing (third-party AI replies billed as service messages from 1 Oct 2026; Meta's own Business Agent billed per token). The clause targets providers distributing an assistant, not businesses using AI incidentally. A single-user personal Claude bot on the Business API is not explicitly addressed and sits in a grey zone of Meta's discretion.

### Cited Findings
- **Scope (Oct 2025):** section 4.7 "AI Providers" bars providers of LLMs, generative AI platforms and general-purpose AI assistants from the Business Solution when AI is the "primary (rather than incidental or ancillary) functionality", as determined by Meta "in its sole discretion". It applied to new users from 15 Oct 2025 and all users from 15 Jan 2026 [confirmed] — summarized in earlier note `cloud_api_coexistence.md` §8, citing [WhatsApp Business Solution Terms](https://www.whatsapp.com/legal/business-solution-terms), [TechCrunch 2025-10-18](https://techcrunch.com/2025/10/18/whatssapp-changes-its-terms-to-bar-general-purpose-chatbots-from-its-platform/), [respond.io](https://respond.io/blog/whatsapp-general-purpose-chatbots-ban)
- **Who left:** Microsoft said Copilot on WhatsApp would stop working on 15 Jan 2026, citing WhatsApp's policy update barring general-purpose chatbots. Copilot ran without sign-in, so WhatsApp chat history could not be migrated [confirmed] — [TechRepublic](https://www.techrepublic.com/article/news-microsoft-copilot-leaving-whatsapp/); [Outlook Business](https://www.outlookbusiness.com/ampstories/corporate/microsoft-copilot-leaving-whatsapp-from-january-15-heres-what-it-means-for-users); [Microsoft Copilot app page](https://www.microsoft.com/copilot-app). OpenAI (ChatGPT, 1-800-CHATGPT), Perplexity and Luzia were also affected [confirmed] — [Gulf News](https://gulfnews.com/technology/no-more-chatgpt-and-perplexity-on-whatsapp-meta-bans-major-ai-chatbots-from-2026-1.500316016). The Commission's account names Luzia and Poke alongside ChatGPT and Perplexity [reported] — [iPhone in Canada, 2026-06-09](https://www.iphoneincanada.ca/2026/06/09/the-eu-just-forced-meta-to-open-whatsapp-to-chatgpt-perplexity/)
- **Meta's stated rationale:** AI chatbots on the Business API strained systems not designed for them, and the API is meant for business-to-customer messaging, not as a distribution platform for general AI models. "App stores and other channels" are the proper route to market [confirmed] — [TechCrunch 2025-10-18](https://techcrunch.com/2025/10/18/whatssapp-changes-its-terms-to-bar-general-purpose-chatbots-from-its-platform/); [Neowin](https://www.neowin.net/news/eu-says-meta-must-restore-rival-chatbots-access-to-whatsapp/). In the EU case Meta argued that many AI distribution options exist (app stores, OSes, devices, websites, partnerships) and disputed that the Business API is a key channel [reported] — [UK Yahoo Finance](https://uk.finance.yahoo.com/news/eu-warns-meta-must-open-093739166.html). After the June 2026 order, Meta called it "regulatory overreach subsidised by the many European companies that pay" and said "We will appeal" (via Reuters) [confirmed] — [Decrypt](https://decrypt.co/370580/eu-orders-meta-open-whatsapp-rival-ai-chatbots). Meta also argued that "Small European businesses shouldn't foot OpenAI's bill" (May 2026) [reported] — [TNW](https://thenextweb.com/news/meta-whatsapp-ai-rivals-free-access-eu-dma-proposal)
- **What is NOT covered:** Meta told TechCrunch the change does not affect businesses using AI to serve their customers (e.g., a travel company's support bot) [confirmed] — [TechCrunch 2025-10-18](https://techcrunch.com/2025/10/18/whatssapp-changes-its-terms-to-bar-general-purpose-chatbots-from-its-platform/); [respond.io](https://respond.io/blog/whatsapp-general-purpose-chatbots-ban). Separately, the terms bar using Business Solution Data to create, develop, train or improve AI models [vendor/blog] — [ServiceForm](https://www.serviceform.com/blog/whatsapp-ai-chatbots-in-2026/)
- **2026 change #1: EEA/Brazil carve-out written into the clause.** Per secondary readers of the terms (versions cited as last modified 6 Mar 2026 and published 16 Aug 2026), the AI-provider clause says such technologies "may be made available to users with phone numbers registered in the European Economic Area or Brazil". The test is the phone number's country code, not the user's location. The UK (+44) is outside the carve-out [vendor/blog, consistent across several] — [ServiceForm](https://www.serviceform.com/blog/whatsapp-ai-chatbots-in-2026/); [Blueticks](https://blueticks.co/blog/chatgpt-whatsapp); [getkanal](https://getkanal.com/blog/chatgpt-whatsapp); [zapvoxia](https://zapvoxia.com.br/en/blog/chatgpt-whatsapp-ban-what-changed). Meta told developers they need not notify or stop serving users with +55 (Brazil) numbers [reported] — [TechCrunch 2026-01-15](https://techcrunch.com/2026/01/15/after-italy-whatsapp-excludes-brazil-from-rival-chatbot-ban); [dig.watch](https://dig.watch/updates/brazil-excluded-from-whatsapp-rival-ai-chatbot-ban)
- **2026 change #2 (23 Sep 2026): carve-out generalized.** A WhatsApp change-log site says that on 23 Sep 2026 the terms were renamed, and the exception no longer names the EEA and Brazil. It now applies "in certain countries that Meta lists in its developer documentation". A second source describes the Sep-23 version as covering only places where Meta is legally required to permit it [vendor/blog, two sources, unverified against Meta's text] — [webonwhatsapp.com change log](https://webonwhatsapp.com/whats-new/) (search extract); [upperfloor.ai](https://upperfloor.ai/en/blog/meta-policy-ai-agents) / [getkanal](https://getkanal.com/blog/chatgpt-whatsapp) (search extracts; exact attribution between these two is unclear)
- **2026 change #3: pricing that separates Meta's AI from third-party AI.** From 1 Aug 2026 Meta bills its own "Meta Business Agent" per token (reported as US$2 per million tokens, about US$0.04 per average reply) [reported] — [Canaltech](https://canaltech.com.br/inteligencia-artificial/ia-que-responde-por-sua-empresa-no-whatsapp-tera-cobranca-por-uso-explica-meta/). From 1 Oct 2026, service messages (free-form replies inside the 24-hour window) become billable on the Business Platform. Meta's developer documentation says a reply "not powered by Meta Business Agent… incurs the charge of a service message", so third-party AI replies are billed per message [confirmed via Meta docs extract plus several vendors] — [Meta developer docs: non-template pricing](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/business-messaging/whatsapp/pricing/non-template-messages); [Wati](https://www.wati.io/en/blog/whatsapp-service-message-pricing/); [Techpoint Africa](https://techpoint.africa/guide/whatsapp-will-charge-businesses-per-message/). Reported details: R$0.035 per service message in Brazil [reported] — [MobileTime 2026-07-01](https://www.mobiletime.com.br/noticias/01/07/2026/whatsapp-cobra-respostas/). The first 1,000 service messages per number per month are free [vendor/blog, unconfirmed] — [ChatMaxima](https://chatmaxima.com/blog/whatsapp-business-updates/); [Chat2Desk](https://chat2desk.com.br/blog/nova-cobranca-whatsapp-outubro-2026-mensagens-de-servico/). MobileTime reports that Meta Business Agent replies are exempt from the per-message service fee (they pay the token fee instead) [reported] — [MobileTime](https://www.mobiletime.com.br/noticias/01/07/2026/whatsapp-cobra-respostas/)
- **AI-provider-specific fee (EU, then Brazil):** on 4 Mar 2026 Meta let general-purpose AI assistants back into the EU for 12 months at €0.0490–€0.1323 per non-template message, depending on country [confirmed] — [TechCrunch 2026-03-05](https://techcrunch.com/2026/03/05/meta-will-allow-rival-ai-chatbots-on-whatsapp-in-europe-but-for-a-fee/); [Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/news/meta-allow-ai-rivals-whatsapp-122729247.html). In Brazil, Meta has charged AI providers per non-template message since 11 Mar 2026 (reported as R$0.31) [vendor/blog] — [Verboo](https://verboo.ai/pt/blog/meta-cobra-mensagem-ia-whatsapp-cade)
- **Personal / single-user bots:** no Meta statement, FAQ or clarification addressing a single individual running their own assistant on a Business API number was found. Public framing consistently targets services "where the chatbot is the product" [gap confirmed by absence] — [TechCrunch 2025-10-18](https://techcrunch.com/2025/10/18/whatssapp-changes-its-terms-to-bar-general-purpose-chatbots-from-its-platform/); [respond.io](https://respond.io/blog/whatsapp-general-purpose-chatbots-ban)

### Inferences
- The Sep-2026 rewrite (if accurate) signals Meta's settled posture: **ban globally, allow only where legally compelled, and keep the list of compelled countries in a document Meta can edit.** Israel is not on any such list, and nothing suggests it will be added voluntarily.
- The pricing shift matters for an Israeli personal-assistant builder even if the clause doesn't bite. Since 1 Oct 2026 every Claude reply sent through the Cloud API is a billable service message (beyond any free tier), while Meta's own agent is billed on a separate meter. For personal low volume the cost is small, but the asymmetry shows that Meta is commercially favoring its own AI.
- **Personal bot reading:** someone who wires Claude to a Business API number that only she messages is plausibly not an "AI Provider" making an assistant "available" to users. But the AI *is* the primary functionality, and Meta decides "in its sole discretion". Enforcement risk is low in practice at very low volume with no public exposure, but it is non-zero and has no appeal path. Sharing the number with family or others would move it closer to "providing" an assistant. (Inference, consistent with the earlier note's §8 analysis.)

### Gaps
- Verbatim current (23 Sep 2026) clause text and the developer-documentation country list could not be fetched (whatsapp.com and developers.facebook.com blocked). Whether Turkey or any other country is on the list is unconfirmed.
- Whether Israel's per-message service-message rate for Oct 2026 has been published was not found.
- No Meta clarification on single-user or personal-use bots exists in the sources found.

## 2. Regulatory pushback: Italy, European Commission (AT.41034), Brazil, Turkey, others, and Israel

### Takeaway
Regulators in four jurisdictions forced Meta to reopen by mid-2026: Italy (interim, Dec 2025, later folded into the EU case), Brazil (Jan 2026; upheld Mar 2026; Meta charges fees), the EU/EEA (interim measures 9 Jun 2026, free access on pre-15-Oct-2025 terms until a final decision; Meta appealed to the General Court in Aug 2026) and Turkey (investigation plus interim measure, 5 Jun 2026). COMESA has also opened a probe. No final infringement decision exists anywhere yet. **No Israeli Competition Authority action or statement was found.**

### Cited Findings
**Italy (AGCM, case A576)**
- AGCM opened its abuse-of-dominance probe in July 2025 (Meta AI integration). It later broadened the probe to the Business Solution terms and on 24 Dec 2025 ordered interim suspension of the clauses excluding rival chatbots. Meta called the decision "fundamentally flawed" and said "We will appeal" [confirmed] — [AGCM interim-measures decision PDF](https://www.agcm.it/dotcmsdoc/allegati-news/A576_adoz%20misure%20cautelari_omi_signed_signed.pdf); [TechCrunch 2025-12-24](https://techcrunch.com/2025/12/24/italy-tells-meta-to-suspend-its-policy-that-bans-rival-ai-chatbots-from-whatsapp/); [Milano Finanza](https://www.milanofinanza.it/news/antitrust-blocca-meta-ai-su-whatsapp-sospese-le-clausole-che-escludono-i-chatbot-concorrenti-202512240857409982)
- **8 Jun 2026: AGCM closed its proceeding without a merits ruling.** The European Commission had extended its own proceeding to Italian territory on 15 Apr 2026, and under Regulation 1/2003 a Commission investigation relieves national authorities of competence over the same conduct. The case now sits in Brussels [reported, single source] — [Teleborsa/ANSA](https://teleborsa.ansa.it/notiziario/finanza/agcm-chiude-istruttoria-su-meta-ai-sara-antitrust-ue-a-decidere-su-abuso-di-posizione-dominante/)

**European Commission (AT.41034)**
- Timeline [confirmed across GCR, CNBC, TechCrunch, Lexology, law firms]: the formal investigation opened 4 Dec 2025 (EEA except Italy) — [The Register 2025-12-04](https://www.theregister.com/2025/12/04/eu_probes_meta_whatsapp_ai/). Meta's fee-based reopening followed on 4 Mar 2026 — [TechCrunch 2026-03-05](https://techcrunch.com/2026/03/05/meta-will-allow-rival-ai-chatbots-on-whatsapp-in-europe-but-for-a-fee/). On 15 Apr 2026 a supplementary SO stated that the fees were equivalent to a ban, and the case was extended to Italy — [ClickOrlando/AP 2026-04-15](https://www.clickorlando.com/tech/2026/04/15/eu-threatens-to-force-meta-to-restore-whatsapp-full-access-for-rival-ai-chatbots/); [Commission IP/26/805 via BetterRegulation](https://service.betterregulation.com/document/851397). Feedback was due 18 May. Poke's maker said it was "far from resolving any of the competition concerns" [reported] — [TNW](https://thenextweb.com/news/meta-whatsapp-ai-rivals-free-access-eu-dma-proposal); [GMA/Reuters](https://www.gmanetwork.com/news/scitech/technology/988279/meta-offers-ai-rival-chatbots-limited-free-whatsapp-access-sources-say/story/); [Bloomberg Gov/Reuters](https://news.bgov.com/artificial-intelligence/meta-offers-ai-rivals-in-europe-free-whatsapp-access-reuters). ### Inferences
- **Remedies under discussion:** (a) free access on pre-Oct-2025 terms (EU interim, Italy interim, Turkey "no technical or economic obstacles"); (b) Meta's counter-model of free access up to a cap and then per-message fees (May 2026 EU offer); (c) fee-based access tolerated in Brazil so far. The final EU decision could impose an access obligation, possibly with FRAND-style pricing, or accept commitments. [speculative]
- **Timelines:** the EU final decision is unlikely before 2027. Commission Art. 102 cases typically run 1.5–3+ years from opening, and this one opened Dec 2025. The General Court appeal of interim measures could produce a suspension ruling within months (the IMS precedent), but none had been reported by mid-Oct 2026. [speculative, based on precedent commentary]
- The regulatory wins are **territorial and number-based**. They protect users with +3x/+4x EEA, +55 and possibly +90 numbers, not +972. Unless Israel acts, an Israeli user gets nothing from these cases, except indirectly if Meta ever decides a global reopening is simpler than a patchwork. Meta's Sep-2026 "country list" approach suggests it prefers the patchwork.

## 3. EU DMA messaging interoperability: can it carry third-party AI into WhatsApp chats? Status and geography

### Takeaway
DMA interoperability went live in the EU in Nov 2025 with two small E2EE messengers (BirdyChat, Haiket), 1:1 only and on EU numbers only, with group chats promised "when partners are ready". It is designed for interpersonal messaging services that match WhatsApp's encryption, not for AI assistants. The AI-access fight is being run under Art. 102 antitrust, not the DMA. It does not apply in Israel and offers no realistic route for a Claude assistant.

### Cited Findings
- Meta announced (Nov 2025) third-party chats in the EU with BirdyChat and Haiket as first partners. Users opt in on Android and iOS and can exchange text, images, voice notes, videos and files. Groups come later. Partners must match WhatsApp's E2EE (Signal Protocol preferred, or equivalent) [confirmed] — [Meta newsroom](https://about.fb.com/news/2025/11/messaging-interoperability-whatsapp-enables-third-party-chats-for-users-in-europe/); [Engadget](https://www.engadget.com/apps/whatsapp-enables-interoperability-with-two-other-messengers-in-the-eu-140000835.html); [TechCrunch 2024-03-06](https://techcrunch.com/2024/03/06/to-comply-with-dma-whatsapp-and-messenger-will-become-interoperable-via-signal/)
- Scope: restricted to WhatsApp accounts registered to EU/European numbers and to mobile apps; web, desktop and tablet are excluded initially. Third parties must sign Meta's reference-offer agreement, and Meta has three months to enable a request [reported] — [Mobile Ecosystem Forum 2025-11-17](https://mobileecosystemforum.com/2025/11/17/whatsapps-first-eu-interoperability-partners-named-but-will-birdychat-and-haiket-move-the-needle/); [UC Today](https://www.uctoday.com/unified-communications/meta-details-whatsapps-interoperability-plan/); [BEREC opinion, Mar 2025](https://www.berec.europa.eu/system/files/2025-03/BoR%20(25)%2021%20BEREC%20Opinion%20on%20Meta's%20reference%20offers.pdf)
- Status Sep 2026: still live with BirdyChat and Haiket. No 2026 announcement of group-chat support or new partners was found [reported] — [TechBuzz (updated Sep 2026)](https://www.techbuzz.ai/articles/whatsapp-opens-cross-platform-messaging-in-europe-as-eu-regulation-kicks-in)
- The WhatsApp AI-provider probe is pursued under Art. 102, not the DMA. ### Inferences
- A third-party AI assistant would need to qualify as an E2EE "number-independent interpersonal communications service" and sign Meta's reference offer. DMA interop therefore does not give a "bring your own AI into my WhatsApp" capability. [inference]
- The DMA is EU-territorial, and interop is restricted to EU-registered numbers. **It has zero applicability to an Israeli +972 account.** [inference from scope above]
- The trend in EU policy (DMA review language, the Android AI decisions) points toward more AI interoperability obligations on gatekeepers over 2027+. But a WhatsApp-specific AI interop mandate is not on any announced agenda. [speculative]

## 4. Meta's enforcement against unofficial clients and bridges in 2026: is it locking down?

### Takeaway
The 2026 trend is technical tightening rather than litigation. Around 30 Jun 2026 a phased server-side passkey/WebAuthn step on companion-device linking broke *new* linking for Baileys, whatsmeow and whatsapp-rust (developer reports; WhatsApp has not confirmed it as anti-bot). Existing sessions are reportedly unaffected. No 2026 Meta lawsuit against unofficial-client developers was found. The terms and Help Center still say unofficial apps lead to bans.

### Cited Findings
- (From earlier note `unofficial_readers_risks.md` §2) The developer-reported passkey/"SHORTCAKE" gate began around 2026-06-30. As of Aug–Sep 2026 the issues were still open with no confirmed upstream fix for headless libraries, and the upstream whatsapp-rust fix "requires a real WebAuthn authenticator, which a headless server can't provide" [reported, developer issue trackers] — [Baileys #2672](https://github.com/WhiskeySockets/Baileys/issues/2672); [zeroclaw #8627](https://github.com/zeroclaw-labs/zeroclaw/issues/8627); [Whapi.Cloud blog (vendor)](https://whapi.cloud/blog/understanding-whatsapp-new-passkey-security-webauthn-explained)
- WABetaInfo (29–30 Jun 2026) described passkey device-linking as a feature *in development* for Android: a confirmation prompt, optional alongside QR, not a replacement [reported] — [WABetaInfo](https://wabetainfo.com/whatsapp-is-working-on-passkey-device-linking-for-android/); [WABetaInfo on X](https://x.com/WABetaInfo/status/2071731714578166090). This conflicts in emphasis with the developer reports of a server-side gate already blocking headless linking.
- Lawsuits found in 2026 concern WhatsApp encryption and privacy claims against Meta (Dawson v. Meta, filed 2026-01-23; Shirazi v. Meta, filed 2026-03-25, reportedly sent to arbitration in Jul 2026). ### Inferences
- The combination is (1) Business API closed to general-purpose AI, (2) the passkey gate on linking, and (3) Business Agent pricing favoring Meta's AI. It amounts to a **de facto squeeze on every unofficial path** a personal AI assistant would use. Whether the passkey gate is anti-automation by intent or a security feature with side effects is unconfirmed. Its effect on headless bridges is the same either way. [inference]
- Lock-down trend: **moderate-to-strong for unofficial clients**, but enforcement remains technical and automated (bans, link failures), not legal action against individuals. [inference]

### Gaps

## 6. Outlook for 2027 and implications for building a Claude assistant on WhatsApp vs. alternatives

### Takeaway
No named analyst published a 2027 forecast specific to this issue. On the evidence, the likely 2027 picture is a **two-tier WhatsApp**: open to third-party AI (free or fee-capped) in the EEA, and likely in Brazil and Turkey, under regulatory compulsion and litigation, and **closed elsewhere, including Israel**, with Meta AI as the only native assistant. For an Israeli builder, a WhatsApp-based Claude assistant carries structural platform risk on both paths. The official Business API path is closed to general-purpose AI and in the grey zone for personal bots, and it now charges per message. The unofficial linked-device path is a ToS breach, with bans and the passkey gate.

### Cited Findings
- Commentators compare Meta's closed AI posture to Apple's App Store walled garden and describe analysts as "split" on impact. No 2027 outlook is given [blog, weak] — [ai2.work](https://ai2.work/blog/whatsapp-ai-lockout-why-the-eu-meta-antitrust-case-matters-in-2026); [Seeking Alpha](https://seekingalpha.com/news/4601911-eu-to-meta-tear-down-this-whatsapp-wall); [TipRanks](https://www.tipranks.com/news/meta-platforms-meta-give-ai-rivals-free-whatsapp-access-heres-why)
- Regulators' rationale: locking more than 3 billion WhatsApp users into Meta AI could give Meta an advantage over rival chatbots, especially smaller entrants [reported] — [UK Yahoo Finance](https://uk.finance.yahoo.com/news/eu-warns-meta-must-open-093739166.html)
- Legal commentary flags that the General Court could suspend interim measures (IMS 2001 precedent) [reported] — [Legal500/Steptoe](https://www.legal500.com/intelligence/belgium/antitrust-competition-law/must-meta-open-whatsapp-to-competing-ai-assistants)
- Meta's own named list of rivals in its May 2026 offer included Anthropic's Claude, i.e., Claude is treated as a potential WhatsApp-distributed assistant in Europe [reported] — [TNW](https://thenextweb.com/news/meta-whatsapp-ai-rivals-free-access-eu-dma-proposal)

### Inferences
- **Scenario A (most likely, 2027):** the EU interim order stands, the EEA stays open, and a final EU decision or commitments land in 2027 or later with some access-pricing regime. Brazil and Turkey stay open with fees. Rest of world, Israel included, stays closed. **Israel: no change.** [speculative]
- **Scenario B:** the General Court suspends the interim measures (a months-scale possibility). Meta reimposes EEA fees or a cap. The direction becomes more closed even in Europe. **Israel: no change.** [speculative]
- **Scenario C (low probability):** Meta finds the patchwork costly and globalizes a capped-free or fee-based access tier for AI providers. Israel would gain *paid* access for providers like OpenAI. A personal Claude bot would still be grey under "sole discretion". [speculative]
- **Israel-specific trigger:** only an Israel Competition Authority case would change the local picture. None is visible as of Oct 2026, and Israeli enforcement against global platforms on this kind of issue has historically been limited. [speculative; no source found]
- **Practical implications for an Israeli user building a Claude personal assistant:**
  - *WhatsApp Business Cloud API with a dedicated number (official):* the most durable WhatsApp option. It is a grey zone under §4.7 for a personal general-purpose assistant, low enforcement risk at personal volume, and now has per-message service fees (from 1 Oct 2026). It cannot read her personal chats. Platform-policy risk: Meta can tighten the clause at any time without notice of appeal.
  - *Unofficial linked-device bridges (Baileys, whatsmeow, Beeper, OpenClaw, MCP servers):* the highest risk. ToS violation, bans, and the passkey gate may block new linking. The trend is getting worse.
  - *Alternatives:* the official Claude apps (iOS, web, desktop), with Anthropic's own connectors for Gmail and Calendar, avoid WhatsApp platform risk entirely. Telegram's Bot API is an officially sanctioned bot channel with no AI-provider ban found in this research. (Telegram was not researched in this session. The report writer should treat it as an unverified alternative.)

### Gaps
- No named analyst or bank 2027 forecasts were found. The scenarios above are the researcher's inference.
- Telegram Bot API policy on AI assistants in 2026 was not researched.
- The timeline for the EU final decision in AT.41034 has not been published.
