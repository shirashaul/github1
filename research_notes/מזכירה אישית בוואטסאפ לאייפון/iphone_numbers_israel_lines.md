## 1. iPhone Dual SIM / eSIM: which models, how many eSIMs, how calls/SMS/data work

### Takeaway
Every eSIM-capable iPhone can hold two active lines at once: physical nano-SIM + eSIM, or two eSIMs on iPhone 13 / SE (3rd gen) and later. It can store more eSIMs than it can use (Apple: "eight or more"), but only two can be active and only one line carries mobile data at a time. iPhones sold in Israel, including iPhone 17 / 17 Pro and iPhone 18 Pro, keep a SIM tray. The iPhone Air and US-bought iPhone 14 or later models are eSIM-only.

### Cited Findings
- "All iPhone models that support eSIM can store multiple eSIMs and use Dual SIM with two active SIMs at the same time" (Apple). — [Apple: Using Dual SIM with an eSIM](https://support.apple.com/en-us/109317)
- Apple says an eSIM-capable iPhone can manage "up to eight or more eSIMs" and activate new plans digitally. These are stored eSIMs; only two can be active at once. — [Apple 109317](https://support.apple.com/en-us/109317); [Apple: Set up eSIM on iPhone](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118669)
- Two active eSIMs (Dual eSIM) are supported on iPhone SE (3rd generation), iPhone 13 and later. The other combination is one physical nano-SIM plus one eSIM. Models without a SIM tray support two active eSIMs. — [Apple 109317](https://support.apple.com/en-us/109317)
- Only one cellular data network can be used at a time. You choose the data line, and you can turn on "Allow Cellular Data Switching" so data follows coverage or the line you are on a call with. Cellular-data switching does not work while Data Roaming is in use. — [Apple 109317](https://support.apple.com/en-us/109317); [Apple iPhone User Guide: Use Dual SIM](https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/use-dual-sim-iph9c5776d3c/ios)
- Both numbers can make and receive voice and FaceTime calls and send and receive iMessage, SMS and MMS. You pick a default line. For each contact, the iPhone reuses the line you last used with that person; otherwise it uses the default. You can switch lines before starting a new iMessage/SMS, but an SMS/MMS conversation cannot be moved to the other line once started. Personal Hotspot uses the data line. — [Apple iPhone User Guide: Use Dual SIM](https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/use-dual-sim-iph9c5776d3c/ios); [Apple 109317](https://support.apple.com/en-us/109317)
- A line can receive calls while the other line is on a call only if Wi‑Fi Calling is turned on for it. — [Apple 109317](https://support.apple.com/en-us/109317)
- To use two different carriers, the iPhone must be unlocked. Otherwise both plans must come from the same carrier. — [Apple 109317](https://support.apple.com/en-us/109317)
- The current Apple page mentions iPhone 18 Pro, iPhone 18 Pro Max, iPhone 17e and iPhone Air in a China-mainland eSIM note, which shows the page is up to date for 2026 models. — [Apple 109317](https://support.apple.com/en-us/109317)
- iPhone 17 / 17 Pro / 17 Pro Max are eSIM-only in 12 markets: US, Canada, Japan, Mexico, Bahrain, Kuwait, Oman, Qatar, Saudi Arabia, UAE, Guam and US Virgin Islands. Elsewhere they have a nano-SIM tray plus eSIM. Israel is not on the eSIM-only list. — [MacRumors, 9 Sep 2025](https://www.macrumors.com/2025/09/09/iphone-17-esim-only-countries-list/); [Wikipedia: iPhone 17](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IPhone_17)
- iPhone Air has no SIM tray in any market (eSIM-only worldwide). — [Saily](https://saily.com/blog/does-iphone-17-have-a-sim-card/); [Holafly](https://esim.holafly.com/news/iphone-17-esim/)
- iPhone 18 Pro (announced 9 Sep 2026) is reported as eSIM-only in the same 12 markets. UK, Europe and most other markets keep a tray, and Israel is not listed as eSIM-only. These are secondary sources; none linked to Apple's country list. Some also say a model called "iPhone Duo" is eSIM-only everywhere, which is unverified. — [The Mac Observer](https://www.macobserver.com/tips/round-ups/iphone-18-pro-esim-only-12-countries-full-list-travelers/); [iThinkDiff](https://www.ithinkdiff.com/iphone-18-pro-esim-only-countries/)
- Israeli carriers: all the large carriers (Pelephone first, then Partner, HOT Mobile and Cellcom) launched eSIM for phones within about a week of each other in 2024. Before that, eSIM was limited to smartwatches. — [Geektime](https://www.geektime.co.il/partner-and-hot-to-offer-esim-services/); [Ynet](https://www.ynet.co.il/economy/article/r1ifmlys6); [TGspot](https://www.tgspot.co.il/israeli-mobile-start-offering-esim-packages/); [Poenta](https://www.poenta.co.il/article/esim-partner-cellcom/); [TheMarker Supermarker eSIM guide](https://www.supermarker.themarker.com/bills/guides/esim-israel)

### Inferences
- Practical setup for the user: keep the current number on the physical SIM (or its existing eSIM) and add a second Israeli line as an eSIM. Both lines then ring, both receive SMS (including WhatsApp codes), and she chooses one for data. No second phone is needed.
- A third line for a bot can sit on the iPhone as a stored but inactive eSIM. It can only receive SMS or calls (for example a WhatsApp/Meta verification code) when switched on, which means temporarily turning off one of the other two lines.
- If her iPhone was bought in the US (iPhone 14 or later), it is eSIM-only. That is still fine for Dual SIM, but both lines must be eSIM and the phone must be carrier-unlocked.
- **[background, unverified]** The first eSIM iPhones were the XS/XR (2018), so almost any iPhone currently in use in Israel supports physical SIM + eSIM Dual SIM.

## 2. Multiple WhatsApp accounts in the same WhatsApp app on iOS (2026)

### Takeaway
Yes. After Android (announced Oct 2023), WhatsApp brought two accounts in one app to iPhone. Beta reports started around Nov 2025, the official announcement came about 27 Mar 2026, and it rolled out gradually through spring 2026. The limit is two accounts, each with its own phone number. Accounts can only be added on the primary phone, and the feature does not exist in the WhatsApp Business app.

### Cited Findings
- Android: WhatsApp announced multiple accounts on one phone in the blog post "Multiple Accounts Coming to WhatsApp" (Oct 2023). — [WhatsApp Blog](https://blog.whatsapp.com/multiple-accounts-coming-to-whatsapp)
- iOS beta reports, "Soon, you can use multiple WhatsApp accounts on same iPhone", date about 19 Nov 2025 (inferred from the URL id). — [Business Standard](https://www.business-standard.com/technology/tech-news/multiple-whatsapp-accounts-on-same-iphone-ios-125111900732_1.html)
- Official launch: the WhatsApp blog roundup "Free up space, multiple accounts, cross-platform transfer and more" says you can now have two WhatsApp accounts logged in at the same time on iOS. Coverage is dated about 27 Mar 2026. — [WhatsApp Blog](https://blog.whatsapp.com/new-feature-roundup-free-up-space-multiple-accounts-cross-platform-transfer-and-more); [Engadget](https://www.engadget.com/social-media/whatsapp-rolls-out-updates-including-multiple-accounts-for-ios-130000252.html); [ALM Corp, 27 Mar 2026](https://almcorp.com/blog/whatsapp-new-features-storage-chat-transfer-iphone-accounts/); [Business Standard, 27 Mar 2026](https://www.business-standard.com/technology/tech-news/whatsapp-adds-multi-account-support-on-ios-adds-more-features-what-s-new-126032700280_1.html)
- Rollout was gradual; not everyone had it right after the announcement. WABetaInfo later reported a "wide" rollout. — [WABetaInfo](https://wabetainfo.com/whatsapp-is-widely-rolling-out-the-multi-account-feature-on-ios/); [Gulf News](https://gulfnews.com/technology/media/whatsapp-rolls-out-multi-account-support-on-iphone-manage-two-numbers-seamlessly-1.500355898)
- How it works: each account keeps its own chats, notifications and settings. An "Account List" in Settings lets you switch, and the active account's profile photo appears in the bottom tab bar. — [Business Standard](https://www.business-standard.com/technology/tech-news/whatsapp-adds-multi-account-support-on-ios-adds-more-features-what-s-new-126032700280_1.html); [Engadget](https://www.engadget.com/social-media/whatsapp-rolls-out-updates-including-multiple-accounts-for-ios-130000252.html)
- Ways to add the second account: register a new number, connect an existing WhatsApp account, or link a "companion" account by scanning a QR code. App Lock (Face ID / Touch ID / passcode) still applies when you switch to a locked account. — [WABetaInfo](https://wabetainfo.com/whatsapp-is-rolling-out-multi-account-feature-on-ios-for-seamless-profile-switching/); [WABetaInfo](https://wabetainfo.com/whatsapp-is-widely-rolling-out-the-multi-account-feature-on-ios/)
- Steps: WhatsApp > Settings > tap the arrow next to your name > "Add account" > verify the second number by SMS or call. — [WhatsApp Blog](https://blog.whatsapp.com/new-feature-roundup-free-up-space-multiple-accounts-cross-platform-transfer-and-more); [WhatsApp FAQ: How to switch accounts](https://faq.whatsapp.com/492167569769444/?cms_platform=web)
- Limits (WhatsApp Help Center):
  - a second account needs a separate phone number;
  - secondary accounts can only be created on your primary mobile device;
  - the feature is not available on linked devices;
  - account switching is not currently available in the WhatsApp Business app.
  
  — [WhatsApp FAQ 492167569769444](https://faq.whatsapp.com/492167569769444/?cms_platform=web)
- The second number can come from a second SIM or eSIM. — [Omnichat blog](https://blog.omnichat.ai/iphone-whatsapp-dual-accounts-setup/)
- Numbers that cannot be registered on regular WhatsApp: VoIP, landline, toll-free, premium-rate, UAN, shared-cost and "personal numbers". Landlines are accepted only in the WhatsApp Business app. — [WhatsApp FAQ: Can't complete registration](https://faq.whatsapp.com/1120385166078156); [WhatsApp FAQ: How to register your phone number](https://faq.whatsapp.com/684051319521343/?helpref=faq_content&cms_platform=android)

### Inferences
- For this user, the simplest split is to keep both personal and work in the one regular WhatsApp app: current number = account 1, new Israeli mobile number (eSIM) = account 2. She switches by tapping her profile photo or using Settings > Account list.
- The second account in regular WhatsApp must be a real mobile number (05x). An 072/073/077 virtual number will not work there (see Q7).
- Make sure WhatsApp is updated from the App Store. If "Add account" is missing, the gradual rollout may not have reached her yet.

### Gaps
- Exact iOS app version and exact rollout completion date not found.
- Not confirmed whether both accounts show notifications with equal reliability in the background on iOS. Sources only say each account "keeps its own notifications".
- Not confirmed whether the maximum on iOS can exceed two. All sources say two.

## 3. WhatsApp (regular) + WhatsApp Business side by side on one iPhone; migrating chats; what contacts see

### Takeaway
The two apps run side by side on one iPhone, but each needs a different number; one number cannot be active in both apps at once. An existing WhatsApp account can be moved into WhatsApp Business with its chat history (one way only), and the WhatsApp Business app also accepts landline/virtual-type numbers verified by voice call. The Business app does not support multiple accounts, so on one iPhone the realistic maximum is 2 accounts in WhatsApp + 1 in WhatsApp Business.

### Cited Findings
- WhatsApp and WhatsApp Business are separate apps. "You can't use the same phone number with each app simultaneously, but it is possible to move between them." — [WhatsApp FAQ: About WhatsApp Business](https://faq.whatsapp.com/641572844337957/?helpref=uf_share); [WhatsApp FAQ: About moving from WhatsApp Messenger to WhatsApp Business](https://faq.whatsapp.com/223728052339165/?locale=en_US)
- To use both apps you need a unique phone number for each. — [WhatsApp FAQ: How to register your phone number](https://faq.whatsapp.com/684051319521343/?helpref=faq_content&cms_platform=android); [WhatsApp FAQ: How to download the WhatsApp Business app](https://faq.whatsapp.com/136038054398590/?locale=en_US)
- Landlines are supported only in the WhatsApp Business app, with the code received on the landline. VoIP, toll-free, premium, UAN and similar numbers are unsupported. — [WhatsApp FAQ 1120385166078156](https://faq.whatsapp.com/1120385166078156); [WhatsApp FAQ 684051319521343](https://faq.whatsapp.com/684051319521343/?helpref=faq_content&cms_platform=android)
- Moving Messenger to Business on the same number: install WhatsApp Business, verify the same number, and allow WhatsApp Business to access your chat history and media when prompted. — [WhatsApp FAQ: How to move your WhatsApp Messenger account to WhatsApp Business](https://faq.whatsapp.com/235676511161201/?locale=en_US)
- One way only: moving everything from WhatsApp Business back to WhatsApp Messenger is not supported. Going back loses business features plus the chats and media created while using Business. — [WhatsApp FAQ 223728052339165](https://faq.whatsapp.com/223728052339165/?locale=en_US)
- WhatsApp's "Move to iOS" Chat Transfer cannot move chats to or from WhatsApp Business. Restoring a backup requires the same number and the same iCloud account. — [WhatsApp FAQ 1195710084347483](https://faq.whatsapp.com/1195710084347483); [WhatsApp FAQ: Back up or restore your chats](https://faq.whatsapp.com/1144861179456352)
- Third-party guidance for iPhone (not official): before switching, back up to iCloud (Settings > Chats > Chat Backup > Back Up Now). Keep WhatsApp Messenger installed while setting up WhatsApp Business. Messages received after the last backup may be missing. — [WhatsAble](https://whatsable.app/blog/migrate-whatsapp-personal-to-business-account-in-2026); [Infobip](https://www.infobip.com/blog/change-whatsapp-to-business-account)
- Account switching (multi-account) is not available in the WhatsApp Business app. — [WhatsApp FAQ 492167569769444](https://faq.whatsapp.com/492167569769444/?cms_platform=web)

### Inferences
- Option A (simplest): personal and work as two accounts in the regular WhatsApp app (Q2).
- Option B: personal in WhatsApp and work in WhatsApp Business. This gives a visual separation (different app icon) and business tools.
  - **[background, unverified]** The Business tools include away/greeting messages, quick replies and labels, which can help limit after-hours work messages.
  - The work number could even be an 072 virtual or landline number verified by voice call, so no second SIM is needed (Q7).
- Option C (maximum): 2 accounts in WhatsApp + 1 in WhatsApp Business on the same iPhone = 3 numbers.
- If she moves her current number into WhatsApp Business to keep all its history there, the move is irreversible (history can't be moved back to regular WhatsApp).
- **[background, unverified]** Contacts chatting with a WhatsApp Business account see a business profile (category, description, hours, etc.). A municipality employee should check whether municipal policy allows using WhatsApp Business for municipal work.

### Gaps
- Could not read the iPhone-specific FAQ steps for Messenger to Business migration verbatim (for example whether iOS transfers directly on-device or via iCloud).
- No official statement found on what contacts see when an existing chat partner switches to Business (labels/notifications).

## 4. Linked Devices / companion mode

### Takeaway
One WhatsApp account (one number) can be used on up to 4 linked devices: phones, iPad, computers and the web. That includes a second phone as a "companion". This is the same account mirrored on several devices, not a second account, so one number really can be on two phones. The primary phone must open WhatsApp at least every 14 days.

### Cited Findings
- You can link up to four devices at a time to your primary phone. Linked phones keep working without the primary phone connected, but you must open WhatsApp on the primary phone every 14 days to keep them linked. — [WhatsApp FAQ: How to link a device](https://faq.whatsapp.com/1324084875126592/?cms_platform=web); [WhatsApp FAQ: How to link a device (Android)](https://faq.whatsapp.com/1317564962315842/?cms_platform=android&lang=en)
- To link a phone: on the primary phone, Settings > Linked devices > Link a device, then scan a QR code or use "link with phone number" and a one-time code. The primary phone shows the device name and location and asks you to confirm. — [WhatsApp FAQ 1324084875126592](https://faq.whatsapp.com/1324084875126592/?cms_platform=web)
- In April 2023 the WhatsApp blog announced linking a phone as one of up to four companion devices. Each linked phone connects independently, and companions are logged out if the primary is inactive for a long period. — [WhatsApp Blog: One WhatsApp account, now across multiple phones](https://blog.whatsapp.com/one-whatsapp-account-now-across-multiple-phones)
- WhatsApp Help Center article: "About using one WhatsApp account on multiple phones, or with multiple phone numbers". — [WhatsApp FAQ 676849476185717](https://faq.whatsapp.com/676849476185717/?locale=en_US)
- Multiple accounts (two accounts on one phone) is a separate feature, needs a separate number, and is not available on linked devices. — [WhatsApp FAQ 492167569769444](https://faq.whatsapp.com/492167569769444/?cms_platform=web)
- On iOS, the second-account slot can be a "companion account" linked by QR, meaning an account whose primary phone is elsewhere. — [WABetaInfo](https://wabetainfo.com/whatsapp-is-rolling-out-multi-account-feature-on-ios-for-seamless-profile-switching/)
- Changing the phone number cannot be done from WhatsApp Desktop or Web, only on the primary device. — [WhatsApp FAQ: How to change your phone number](https://faq.whatsapp.com/498754122134583/?cms_platform=iphone&helpref=platform_switcher)

### Inferences
- "Same number on two phones" = yes, via companion mode. One phone is primary (holds the registration); the other is linked and counts toward the 4-device limit.
- Linking a device does not need a new number or SIM and does not create a separate identity.
- A practical pattern: work account primary on her iPhone, linked to the municipal office PC (WhatsApp Desktop/Web), so work chats can stay at the office.
- A WhatsApp number registered on the Cloud API for a bot cannot also be used in WhatsApp Messenger (Q8). Companion mode does not let the API bot number appear in her WhatsApp app (except Business-app Coexistence, Q8).

### Gaps
- Did not confirm whether the 14-day rule and the 4-device limit changed in 2026. The FAQ text found still says 4 devices and 14 days.

## 5. Moving only some contacts/groups to a new work number; migration tips

### Takeaway
WhatsApp's "Change Number" moves the whole account (profile, all chats, all groups) to a new number and automatically notifies every group, so it cannot split one account in two. To split personal from work, keep the old account for one side and open a fresh account on the new number for the other. Then move the relevant contacts and groups over manually.

### Cited Findings
- Change Number (Settings > Account > Change Number) migrates "your account information (including your profile information) and your groups" to the new number. — [WhatsApp FAQ: How to change your phone number](https://faq.whatsapp.com/498754122134583/?cms_platform=iphone&helpref=platform_switcher)
- "Notify contacts" is optional, with three choices: All contacts / Contacts I have chats with / Custom. — [WhatsApp FAQ 498754122134583](https://faq.whatsapp.com/498754122134583/?cms_platform=iphone&helpref=platform_switcher)
- Your group chats are automatically notified when you change number, whether or not you choose to notify contacts. Blocked contacts stay blocked and are not notified. — [WhatsApp FAQ 498754122134583](https://faq.whatsapp.com/498754122134583/?cms_platform=iphone&helpref=platform_switcher)
- Change Number must be done on the primary phone, not on Desktop or Web. — [WhatsApp FAQ 498754122134583](https://faq.whatsapp.com/498754122134583/?cms_platform=iphone&helpref=platform_switcher)
- Cloud backups tied to the old number can no longer be used to restore after a number change. Restoring needs the same number and cloud account. — [WhatsApp FAQ: About changing phones](https://faq.whatsapp.com/1197347060992858/?cms_platform=android); [WhatsApp FAQ: Back up or restore](https://faq.whatsapp.com/1144861179456352)

### Inferences
These are practical tips derived from the facts above.
1. **Decide which side keeps the existing number.**
   - Usually keep it for personal life (family and friends) and give work the new number, because work contacts are more defined (colleagues, residents, contractors, municipal groups) and can be told formally.
   - The reverse is also possible if many residents or colleagues know the current number. In that case, use Change Number on the personal side, which also auto-notifies all groups.
2. **Do not use Change Number to split.** It moves everything and announces the change to all groups.
3. **Add the new number as a second account** (WhatsApp Settings > arrow next to name > Add account), or register it in WhatsApp Business.
4. **Tell work contacts yourself.** Send a short message from the new number ("This is my new work WhatsApp number; please save it and use it for municipal matters"), plus a one-line notice from the old number to each work chat. Consider adding the new number to the email signature and to the municipality's contact list.
5. **Groups.**
   - Where she is admin, add the new work number to each work group, make it admin, then leave with the old number.
   - Where she is not admin, ask the group admin to add the new number and remove the old one.
   - Personal groups stay on the old number.
6. **Keep records.** Before leaving work groups on the old number, keep a backup or export of any chat with municipal records value.
   - **[background, unverified]** WhatsApp's "Export chat" creates a text/zip file. It does not move history into another account.
7. **WhatsApp Business only:** away/greeting messages can tell senders which number to use. **[background, unverified]** These can be limited to selected recipients.

### Gaps
- No official WhatsApp feature was found that moves selected chats or groups from one account to another. All sources describe only whole-account moves.
- Municipal or Israeli public-sector rules on using personal WhatsApp for work were not researched (outside scope).

## 7. Israeli virtual/VoIP numbers (072/073/074/077) and landlines for WhatsApp / WhatsApp Business / Cloud API

### Takeaway
Regular WhatsApp officially rejects VoIP and landline numbers. The WhatsApp Business app accepts landlines with a voice-call code, and Meta's Cloud API accepts fixed-line numbers with voice OTP. Israeli virtual-number providers (072/074 and others) market WhatsApp Business registration by voice call at about ₪10–₪39/month. A virtual number therefore suits the WhatsApp Business app or an API bot, not the second account in regular WhatsApp.

## 8. A number used only for an API bot (WhatsApp Business Platform / Cloud API): can it live without a phone?

### Takeaway
The Cloud API runs on Meta's servers, so no phone runs WhatsApp for the bot. The number only has to receive one SMS or voice code during registration (a landline or virtual number works by voice). While registered on the API it cannot also be used in regular WhatsApp. The exception is the WhatsApp Business app via "Coexistence".

Meta does not explicitly say the SIM can be discarded afterwards, so keep the line paid and the 2FA PIN safe. The bot will need billing: from 1 Oct 2026, service messages are charged (Israel about $0.0053 each, with about 1,000 free per number per month). Since 15 Jan 2026, general-purpose AI chatbots are banned on the WhatsApp Business Platform.

### Cited Findings
**Eligibility and registration**
- Eligible numbers must:
  - be owned by you;
  - have a country and area code (short codes are not supported);
  - be able to receive voice calls or SMS;
  - have "scaled capabilities".
  
  The code is sent by SMS or VOICE (the requester picks). — [Meta for Developers: Business phone numbers](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/business-messaging/whatsapp/business-phone-numbers/phone-numbers); [Meta: Registering phone numbers](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/whatsapp/solution-providers/phone-numbers/registering-phone-numbers/)
- Registration is four steps: create the number on a WhatsApp Business Account (WABA), request a code, verify it, then call "register" for the Cloud API. Verification alone does not register the number. — [Meta: Business phone numbers](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/whatsapp/cloud-api/phone-numbers)
- "Registered numbers can still be used for everyday purposes, such as calling and text messages, but cannot be used with WhatsApp Messenger." — [Meta: Business phone numbers](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/whatsapp/cloud-api/phone-numbers)
- A two-step-verification PIN (6 digits) is required when registering, and is needed to change the PIN or delete the number from the platform. Meta is gradually phasing out the PIN for eligible Cloud API numbers. — [Meta: Two-Step Verification](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/business-messaging/whatsapp/business-phone-numbers/two-step-verification/); [Meta: Business phone numbers](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/business-messaging/whatsapp/business-phone-numbers/phone-numbers)
- New business portfolios are initially limited to 2 registered business phone numbers. This rises automatically to 20 once the business is verified or reaches a 2,000 messaging limit. — [Meta: Business phone numbers](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/whatsapp/cloud-api/phone-numbers)

**Coexistence (Business app + API on one number)**
- "Coexistence" (Meta's "Onboard WhatsApp Business app users") lets one number run in the WhatsApp Business app and the Cloud API at the same time. — [Meta: Onboard WhatsApp Business app users](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/business-messaging/whatsapp/embedded-signup/onboarding-business-app-users/); [WATI](https://support.wati.io/en/articles/11822402-introducing-whatsapp-coexistence); [YCloud](https://www.ycloud.com/blog/whatsapp-business-app-coexistence-meta-update)
- Before Coexistence, moving to the API meant removing the number from the app and losing chat history, groups, calls, status and communities. — [LeadSquared](https://www.leadsquared.com/learn/marketing/whatsapp-coexistence/)
- Vendor claim: the number should have been active in the Business app for at least 7 days (1–2 months recommended). — [Whautomate](https://whautomate.com/whatsapp-coexistence)
- An API-live number must be deleted from WhatsApp Manager before it can be onboarded through the Business app. — [Spur help](https://help.spurnow.com/en/articles/12781620-how-to-use-whatsapp-api-business-app-at-the-same-time)

**Does the SIM need to stay?**
- No authoritative Meta statement was found that the SIM can be removed after verification. A third-party consumer-app guide says the SIM is needed only for the one-time code, but that is about the consumer app, not the API. — [Alohaa](https://www.alohaa.ai/blog/how-to-create-a-whatsapp-account-without-a-sim-card); [WATI prerequisites](https://www.wati.io/en/blog/whatsapp-api-prerequisites/); [Callbell requirements](https://callbellsupport.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/13684222094620-Mandatory-requirements-for-WhatsApp-API-connection)

**Pricing change, 1 Oct 2026**
- Service messages (free-form replies within the 24-hour window, including chatbot replies) and utility messages sent inside the window become billable.
- Israel rate: about $0.0053 per delivered message, the same as the utility rate.
- A free tier of 1,000 service messages per business phone number per month. ⚠ One blog headline mentions 2,000; verify with Meta.
- No volume discounts on service messages.
- A payment method had to be on file by 30 Sep 2026, or service messages stop being delivered.

**AI chatbot policy**
- General-purpose AI chatbots (for example ChatGPT, Perplexity, Luzia, Poke) have been barred from the WhatsApp Business Platform since 15 Jan 2026; new users have been subject to the rule since 15 Oct 2025. Businesses using AI incidentally (support bots, order status) are generally not covered. — [respond.io](https://respond.io/blog/whatsapp-general-purpose-chatbots-ban); [Gulf News](https://gulfnews.com/technology/no-more-chatgpt-and-perplexity-on-whatsapp-meta-bans-major-ai-chatbots-from-2026-1.500316016); [Alibaba Cloud guide](https://www.alibabacloud.com/help/en/chatapp/use-cases/whatsapp-ai-policy-2026-guide)

### Inferences
**Recommended setup for the bot number**
- Use a cheap Israeli eSIM line (about ₪16–₪22/month, Q6) or an 072 virtual number (about ₪10–₪39/month, Q7, voice OTP). Register it once in Meta's WhatsApp Manager / Cloud API, set and save the 2FA PIN, and the bot then runs with no phone involved.
- Keep the line paid even though it is idle. Carriers recycle unpaid numbers, and you may need a new OTP if you re-register, move to another provider or (re)enable Coexistence.
- If the bot line is an eSIM on her iPhone, it can stay stored but switched off. Turn it on only when a code is needed.

**Number reuse**
- Do not reuse her personal or work WhatsApp number for the API bot. That number would leave WhatsApp Messenger, unless it is a WhatsApp Business app number onboarded through Coexistence.

**Policy risk**
- A personal "AI secretary" built on a general LLM sits near the Meta AI-provider ban. A bot serving her own work or her own business is more likely "incidental", but this is uncertain and should be checked against Meta's current WhatsApp Business Solution Terms.

### Gaps
- Meta's documentation could not be read in full. No explicit Meta statement found on whether an API-registered number stays valid if the carrier line is cancelled. Re-verification triggers are also unclear.
- Exact October 2026 Israel rates and the free-tier size should be confirmed on Meta's pricing page. Third-party sources conflict on 1,000 vs 2,000 free messages.
- Whether Meta treats a single-user personal AI-assistant bot as a "general-purpose AI chatbot" under the 2026 policy was not determined.
